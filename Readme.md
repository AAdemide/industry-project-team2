# BizBot.AI
**Boost Efficiency. Cut Costs. Grow Smarter with AI.**

🚀 **Built with Gemini AI API**

## Overview
BizBot.AI is an AI-powered assistant designed to help small businesses streamline operations, reduce costs, and optimize decision-making. By leveraging Microsoft Copilot, our platform delivers personalized business insights, task automation, and tailored recommendations to enhance productivity.

## Features
- ✅ **AI-Powered Business Insights** – Get actionable recommendations based on your unique business needs.
- ✅ **Microsoft Tool Optimization** – Smart suggestions for integrating Microsoft 365, Teams, and more.
- ✅ **Automated Action Plans** – AI-driven strategies to improve efficiency and cut unnecessary costs.
- ✅ **Explainable AI** – Transparent decision-making to build trust and minimize bias.

## Ethical Commitment
We recognize the challenges of AI bias and inclusivity. BizBot.AI incorporates explainable AI to ensure transparency, fairness, and reliability in all recommendations.
